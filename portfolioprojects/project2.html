Usability Testing Round 1

Scenarios

My team and I conducted moderated usability tests with 5 users. They were presented with 3 scenarios; go through onboarding, log a reflection for a recently watched movie, and explore a shelf’s view and add a new item to it.

  • Scenario 1: Imagine you have just downloaded Mosaic for the very first time. Please proceed through the onboarding process in the way that feels most natural to you.
  • Scenario 2: Imagine you recently watched the movie Project Hail Mary and have just opened the app to write a reflection on it. Use the prototype to log a new entry in the way that feels most natural to you.
  • Scenario 3: Imagine you want to add Dune: Part One to your Sci-Fi list. Use the prototype in the way that feels most natural to you and add a new movie to your previously made list.
