UX Designer and Researcher with a background in filmmaking. Based in Chicago, Illinois.

Bio

My path started on film sets as a gaffer and key grip. Whether I was rigging equipment or controlling lighting, my job was to understand technical constraints and shape how an audience perceived a scene. Today, I translate that technical problem-solving and eye for visual hierarchy into digital spaces. I map out user needs through deep research and design clean, accessible interfaces where every element has a purpose. I recently earned my masters in Human-Computer Interaction.
